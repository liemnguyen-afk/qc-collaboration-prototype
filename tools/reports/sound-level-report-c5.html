Conclusion

Two of the five units (S/N XR3-0413 and XR3-0415) exceed the 95 dB(A) upper limit, so lot B-2214 is non-conforming to characteristic 5. Teardown of XR3-0413 measured fiberglass packing density at 78 kg/m³ against the 96 kg/m³ specification, which is consistent with the elevated tailpipe level and with the insertion loss margin recorded on characteristic 6.

Recommendation: re-pack the affected lot to 96 kg/m³ and re-test all five units per SAE J1169 before shipment. No shell, weld or dimensional cause was identified.
