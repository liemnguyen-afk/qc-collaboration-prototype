Revision note (Rev. 1) — The initial run on 06/10/2026 reported 359.1 mm on unit XR3-0413, outside the 358.00 mm upper limit. During verification, fixture datum B was found shifted by 1.7 mm. Conclusion

All five units conform to the body length and tube OD requirements of drawing EX-4471 rev. C. Body length is biased toward the upper tolerance limit — the lot mean sits 0.7 mm above nominal and Cpk on the upper side is 0.67, below the 1.33 target — so the process is capable but not centred.

Recommendation: adjust the shell cut length by −0.7 mm and re-verify on the first 5 units of the next lot. Fixture datum B has been re-shimmed and added to the daily gauge check sheet.
